# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: December 12, 2025**

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Stochastic ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our Service.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide
- **Account Information**: Email address (if you create an account)
- **Compositions**: Audio graphs and projects you save to cloud storage (optional)
- **User Settings**: Preferences and configuration settings

### 2.2 Automatically Collected Information
- **Usage Data**: Anonymous analytics about how you use the Service
- **Browser Information**: Browser type, version, and capabilities
- **Performance Data**: Loading times, errors, and performance metrics

### 2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect
- We do NOT collect your compositions unless you explicitly save them to cloud storage
- We do NOT track your audio output or exported files
- We do NOT access your AI API keys (stored only in your browser)
- We do NOT collect personal information beyond what's necessary

## 3. How We Use Your Information

### 3.1 Service Provision
- Authenticate your account
- Store and sync your cloud-saved compositions
- Provide customer support
- Communicate service updates

### 3.2 Service Improvement
- Analyze usage patterns to improve features
- Identify and fix bugs
- Optimize performance
- Develop new features

### 3.3 Legal Compliance
- Comply with legal obligations
- Protect against fraud or abuse
- Enforce our Terms of Service

## 4. Data Storage

### 4.1 Local Storage
- By default, compositions are stored locally in your browser
- Local data never leaves your device
- You control local data through browser settings

### 4.2 Cloud Storage (Optional)
- Cloud features use Supabase for authentication and storage
- Stored data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
- You can delete cloud-stored data at any time
- Cloud storage is subject to Supabase's privacy policy

### 4.3 Data Location
- Cloud data may be stored on servers in various jurisdictions
- Supabase data centers comply with industry-standard security practices

## 5. Third-Party Services

### 5.1 AI Services
- AI features require your own API keys (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, etc.)
- API keys are stored only in your browser's local storage
- We never see or store your API keys on our servers
- Communication with AI services is direct from your browser
- Each provider has their own privacy policy

### 5.2 Authentication
- We use Supabase for authentication
- Authentication data is handled according to Supabase's privacy policy
- See: https://supabase.com/privacy

### 5.3 Analytics (If Applicable)
- We may use privacy-focused analytics tools
- Analytics are anonymous and do not identify individual users
- You can opt out of analytics in your browser settings

## 6. Data Sharing

### 6.1 What We Share
- We do NOT sell your personal information
- We do NOT share your compositions without your permission
- We may share anonymous, aggregated usage statistics

### 6.2 Service Providers
- We may share data with service providers who help operate the Service
- Service providers are bound by confidentiality obligations
- Examples: Hosting (Vercel, Netlify), Storage (Supabase)

### 6.3 Legal Requirements
We may disclose information if required by law or in response to:
- Valid legal process (subpoena, court order)
- Protection of our legal rights
- Investigation of fraud or security issues
- Protection of user safety

## 7. Data Security

### 7.1 Security Measures
- HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data transmission
- Secure authentication through Supabase
- Regular security audits of code and dependencies
- Protection against common web vulnerabilities

### 7.2 Your Responsibility
- Keep your account credentials secure
- Use strong, unique passwords
- Log out from shared devices
- Report security issues immediately

### 7.3 Limitations
- No system is completely secure
- We cannot guarantee absolute security
- You use the Service at your own risk

## 8. Your Rights

### 8.1 Access and Control
- Access your account information
- Download your cloud-stored compositions
- Update your account settings
- Delete your account and data

### 8.2 Data Portability
- Export your compositions in standard formats (JSON, WAV, MIDI)
- Transfer compositions between devices
- No vendor lock-in

### 8.3 Right to Delete
- Delete individual compositions from cloud storage
- Delete your entire account
- Request deletion of all associated data

### 8.4 Opt-Out Rights
- Decline cloud storage features (use local storage only)
- Opt out of analytics (if implemented)
- Unsubscribe from email communications

## 9. Children's Privacy

- Stochastic is not directed at children under 13
- We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13
- If we learn we have collected such information, we will delete it
- Parents/guardians can contact us to request deletion

## 10. International Users

If you are in the European Economic Area, you have additional rights:
- Right to access your personal data
- Right to rectification of inaccurate data
- Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
- Right to restrict processing
- Right to data portability
- Right to object to processing
- Right to withdraw consent

Contact us to exercise these rights.

## 11. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents have the right to:
- Know what personal information is collected
- Know whether personal information is sold or disclosed
- Opt-out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell data)
- Request deletion of personal information
- Non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights
